SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH EXPERIENCE & THEMES

Endocrinology, Diabetes, and Metabolism
Children’s Hospital Los Angeles
In my current role at CHLA, I serve as the lead biostatistician on multiple longitudinal clinical studies investigating how diet influences glycemic control in adolescents, children, and infants. I integrate continuous glucose monitoring data, dietary data, and statistical modeling to evaluate metabolic outcomes. In parallel, I work as a data engineer, managing data frames for several multi-institutional clinical studies through programming and data infrastructure maintenance.		 2024 - Present
Microbial Genomics & Ecology
University of California, Santa Barbara
During my Ph.D., I investigated how marine biofilms develop under varying environmental conditions across ocean depths. I assessed depth-specific community formation by analyzing changes in bacterial diversity over time using 16S rRNA sequencing, amplicon sequence variants, statistical modeling, and bioinformatics. I also generated metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs) to characterize the metabolic capacity of individual bacteria, with a focus on carbohydrate catabolism through CAZyme analysis.		 2018 - 2024
Natural Product Discovery
Zymergen Inc. (acquired by Ginkgo Bioworks)
As part of a bioinformatics research group, I analyzed long-read soil microbiome sequences to identify biosynthetic gene clusters with potential to produce novel, patentable secondary metabolites. I used computational approaches including metagenomic assembly, predictive metabolic software, and custom coding to characterize these clusters. This work supported downstream investigation and validation of high-value metabolites.		 2021
Quantitative Ecology
California State University, Los Angeles
I used fieldwork, remote sensing, and statistical modeling to assess how the Los Angeles urban forest supports wildlife across socioeconomic gradients in the Los Angeles basin. I used birds as model organisms to evaluate how native and non-native street trees provide ecosystem resources to urban wildlife. Specifically, I identified which non-native trees offer resources comparable to native species and analyzed how socioeconomic factors influence urban bird diversity.		 2016 - 2018
Developmental & Molecular Biology
California Institute of Technology (CalTech)
I used plasmid cloning, bacterial transformation, immunohistochemistry, embryology, and live-cell confocal imaging targeting green fluorescent protein to investigate neural crest cell migration during chicken embryonic development. My work focused on visualizing cellular movement in vivo. Specifically, I examined how neural crest cells migrate toward developing sensory organs.		 2015
Cell & Molecular Biology
California State University, Los Angeles
I used molecular techniques including restriction enzyme digestion, plasmid cloning, bacterial transformations, transient transfections, and luciferase reporter assays to investigate actin expression in developing skeletal muscle cells. My work focused on quantifying transcriptional regulation in myoblasts. Specifically, I evaluated how MyoD influences actin expression during muscle development.		 2013 - 2015
